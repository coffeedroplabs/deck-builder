H1 2026 memo for the board - Kestrel Home Goods

Revenue: A$4.20m in H1 against a plan of A$4.60m, so we're 9% behind. Every month grew,
just not as fast as plan. The gap is mostly wholesale: two retail partners delayed their
spring orders to Q3.

Margin: gross margin went from 38.0% in Jan to 42.0% in Jun, mostly the freight contract we
renegotiated in March.

Cash: A$4.83m at the end of June. Average monthly burn was about A$214k over H1, which gives
roughly 22 months at the current rate. (Burn = fall in the cash balance, Jan to Jun, over 5 months.)

Customers: active customers up from 1,210 to 1,431 (+18%).

DECISION NEEDED AT THE MEETING: approve A$600k capex for the second warehouse (Brisbane)
to open in Q4 2026, or delay it to Q2 2027.
- Approve: cuts delivery time to QLD from 4 days to 1, ops estimate +A$900k revenue a year
  once running; runway drops to about 20 months.
- Delay: keeps runway at ~22 months, but QLD orders keep the 4-day delivery; the landlord
  will only hold the site until 30 Nov.
- Management recommends approving.

Risks: wholesale timing again in H2; freight contract is up for renewal in Feb 2027.

Sources: monthly management accounts (Xero), ops forecast for Brisbane (Aug 2026).
